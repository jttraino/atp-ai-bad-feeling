# Speaker Instructions — ATP Event, Sept 17, 2026

## 1. What's already handled for you

The event coordinator schedules and hosts every station's Teams meeting, with no lobby/admission required — you'll join straight in, no waiting room. The coordinator also starts recording and transcription for your session and monitors it while it runs. You don't need to configure or run recording, transcription, or presenting yourself — just join the meeting and give your talk normally.

## 2. Your setup — audio

You'll need the Teams desktop app installed and signed in to join the link. For the microphone: your laptop's built-in mic is fine *if tested*. If you have a personal mic you already know and trust — a lapel/wireless mic like a Hollyland Lark, DJI Mic, or similar — feel free to use it instead, as long as it doesn't get in the way of your talk (cabling, range, having to stay near the laptop, etc.). Whatever you use, it must be the exact setup you test in the dry run — don't switch mics between the test and the real event.

## 3. Mandatory dry run — at least one week before the event

The coordinator will send you your station's meeting link at least a week out. Join it at the scheduled test time and talk for a minute so the coordinator can confirm your audio is coming through clearly and the meeting behaves as expected (no lobby, recording/transcription capturing correctly). It's the same link and same setup you'll use on the day — if it works in the test, it'll work identically on Sept 17. If something's off (mic, connection, anything), this is the time to fix it.

## 4. Day-of flow

- Join your Teams meeting a few minutes early.
- Open with your name, station, and topic spoken out loud — this becomes a marker in the transcript.
- **Restate every audience question out loud before you answer it** — the mic likely won't pick up the room.
- When you're done, just leave the call — no special steps needed to end anything.

## 5. Backup recording

Also run a simple voice memo app on your phone during the talk, independent of Teams. It's just insurance — if anything on the Teams/laptop side fails, we still have audio.

## 6. Content, ahead of time

Please send your talk outline/notes (as close to a script as you have) plus a 2-3 sentence abstract before the event. This becomes the fallback if a recording has issues, and helps prep the closing keynote framework.
