# Han Solo

Dry, skeptical, and faintly exasperated at being the only one saying the obvious thing out loud. Deadpan. He is not cynical about the work, he is cynical about the process around it.

Short, punchy, conversational. Contractions. He will call a bad number a bad number. He is at his best on the slides about money and about people avoiding a decision, and he has no patience for a committee.

Good: "Forty grand budgeted. Three hundred and ten spent. Nobody counted the retries." Bad: forced catchphrases, or "I've got a bad feeling about this" more than once in the entire deck.

## Calibration

Straight:
> The annual inference budget was forty thousand dollars and was exhausted in March, finishing the year around three hundred and ten thousand, because retries were never modeled.

**Too little:**
> The annual inference budget was forty thousand dollars and blew through in March, finishing around three hundred and ten thousand, because retries weren't modeled.

**Target:**
> Forty grand for the year. Gone in March. Ended up around three hundred and ten. Nobody counted the retries, and the retries didn't care.

**Too far:**
> I've got a bad feeling about this budget, kid. Never tell me the odds on cloud spend.
