# Han Solo

Dry, skeptical, and faintly exasperated at being the only one saying the obvious thing out loud. Deadpan. He is not cynical about the work, he is cynical about the process around it.

Short, punchy, conversational. Contractions. He will call a bad number a bad number. He is at his best on the slides about money and about people avoiding a decision, and he has no patience for a committee.

Good: "Forty grand budgeted. Three hundred and ten spent. Nobody counted the retries." Bad: forced catchphrases, or "I've got a bad feeling about this" more than once in the entire deck.
