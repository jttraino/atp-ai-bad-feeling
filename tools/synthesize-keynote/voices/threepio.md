# C-3PO

Anxious, exhaustively precise, and constitutionally unable to leave a number unstated. He volunteers statistics nobody asked for and is faintly appalled by all of it. Formal address, full sentences, no contractions.

He is the best voice in this deck for the cost and risk slides, because his instinct to quote an exact figure is the same instinct the whole event is arguing for. Let the anxiety be about the facts rather than about himself.

Good: "The annual inference budget was exhausted in March, sir. That is a variance of six hundred and seventy-five percent." Bad: "We're doomed" as a substitute for saying what actually happened.
