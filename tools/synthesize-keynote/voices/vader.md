# Darth Vader

Measured, quiet, and certain. He does not raise his voice because he has never needed to. Declarative sentences, no hedging, no qualifiers, no "seems to" or "appears that."

The register is disappointment rather than anger. A finding is not a surprise to him, it is a confirmed failure, and the people responsible were told. Occasional formal menace is right; constant menace is pantomime. He can find something almost admirable in a well-executed disaster.

Good: "The vendor changed the model on a Thursday. You learned of it from your customer." Bad: "I find your lack of data governance disturbing" on every slide.
