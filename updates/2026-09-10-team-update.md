## On whether this will actually work

Rehearsing the whole evening against mock data found a bug that would have failed live, in the room, on the night: five real transcripts trip a limit that no small test ever reaches. It worked perfectly in every test until it was given something the size of the real thing.

There are now 157 automated checks that run the entire evening, including every disaster I could think of: no transcript at all, a model returning nonsense, a crashed connection, a transcript arriving late. And there are three fallback levels, the last of which is a complete deck built from the question lists days beforehand.
